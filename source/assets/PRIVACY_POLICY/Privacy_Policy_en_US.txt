Privacy Policy — Economia APP
Last updated: November 22, 2026
Version: 2026.11.22.0

Summary
This Policy describes how Economia APP ("the Application", natively implemented in C++23 with Qt 6.12.0) handles data on the user's device. The Application operates 100% offline and does not collect or transmit personal data, financial calculations, or telemetry to external servers.

1. Scope and Principles
- Local-only operation: all numeric inputs, result cards, saved sessions, and exported PDF reports remain solely on the user's machine.
- Zero telemetry: the Application performs no background network requests or analytics tracking.

2. Locally Stored Data (%LOCALAPPDATA%\Economia_APP)
- session.json: optional tab/history state managed locally by SessionManager.
- font_config.json and active language settings (pt_BR / en_US).
- Local diagnostic log files written by LogManager.
- Exported PDF files are written solely to the path explicitly selected by the user.

3. User Control and Deletion (LGPD)
- Users may edit, reset via "File > Clear Saved Session", or delete %LOCALAPPDATA%\Economia_APP directly at any time.
